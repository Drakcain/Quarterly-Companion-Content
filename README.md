# Sunday School Study content

Static delivery of 35 application-pinned, verified God's Acres Adult Sunday School quarterly packages (456 lessons). These files are used by Sunday School Study's user-initiated downloader. The Android application independently verifies its pinned manifest, package SHA-256, size, schema and source identities before installation.

Original lesson wording and source-specific author, adaptation, publisher and copyright notices are preserved inside each package. Many lessons are adapted from Mary Lehmann with the applicable Bible Paths Publications notices. Sheri Strelecky and Earl R. Borders are credited where named; original compiler/editor credits remain.

Written permission to distribute the Adult Sunday School materials through Sunday School Study was provided by Sister Sheri Strelecky in September 2026, subject to preserving original content and properly crediting the authors. This is a public summary supplied by the owner, not publication of private correspondence or a grant to alter teaching content. Sunday School Study is free of charge.

No app source, credentials, private correspondence, original PDF binaries, databases, user study data, backups or audio models are included.
